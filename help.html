Help

Keyboard Controls

Move

  • ← Left Arrow: move left
  • → Right Arrow: move right

Jump

  • Space or ↑ Up Arrow: jump

Throw

  • D: throw a bottle

Note: The game listens for d and D for throwing.

Mobile Controls (Touch)

On mobile/tablet you play with the on-screen buttons:

Move left
Move right
Jump
Throw bottle

How to Play

  • Collect coins to increase your progress.
  • Collect bottles so you can throw them.
  • Defeat chickens by jumping on them from above.
  • Defeat the endboss by hitting him with thrown bottles.

Options

  • Audio: turn sound on/off
  • Choose Theme: select your visual theme (e.g. Blue)
  • Choose Difficulty: select difficulty (e.g. Hard)
  • Count of Enemies: set how many enemies spawn
  • Count of minimum Coins: set the minimum number of coins in the level
  • Save: save your settings
